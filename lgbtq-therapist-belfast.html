Queer affirming therapy with complete privacy in Belfast

Belfast has a vibrant LGBTQ+ community, yet local social circles can sometimes feel remarkably small and interconnected. Finding therapy where you feel confident about total discretion is essential. Online therapy provides a quiet, private space where you can speak openly about your life, relationships, and identity from the comfort of your own home, without worrying about bumping into acquaintances in a local waiting room.

Working online also removes the practical stress of travelling across the city after a demanding day. You can sit in a space where you feel safe and relaxed, taking time after each session to process your thoughts without immediately jumping into public transport or busy streets. It offers a calm buffer between deep therapeutic work and your daily routine.

Lived experience and a person centred approach

As a gay man, my practice is built on lived experience of navigating coming out, identity, family dynamics, and self-acceptance. You will not need to educate your therapist about queer culture, terminology, or relationship structures. My foundation is person-centred therapy, which means I offer unconditional positive regard, empathy, and a relationship built on trust and genuine acceptance.

Alongside person-centred work, I integrate somatic experiencing to help release physical tension caused by stress or shame. You can find out more about my dedicated LGBTQ+ affirming therapy or my work as an online therapist for gay men. I work with individuals of all gender identities and sexualities across Belfast and Northern Ireland.

Addressing common concerns for LGBTQ+ individuals

Many queer clients coming to therapy carry subtle or heavy layers of minority stress, internalised shame, or exhaustion from masking their true selves. You might be navigating family tension, exploring gender identity, dealing with relationship challenges, or simply seeking a space to talk about life without your identity being treated as a problem to be solved.

Therapy is a place to explore whatever matters most to you, whether that is directly related to your LGBTQ+ identity or entirely separate. We move at a pace that feels comfortable for you, building self-worth, emotional resilience, and a deeper sense of internal ease in an environment that honors your lived reality.

Questions about LGBTQ+ therapy in Belfast

Why choose online LGBTQ+ therapy in Belfast?

Online therapy ensures total privacy and convenience for clients living in Belfast. You can access specialized queer-affirming therapy from home without worrying about local waiting rooms or commuting across the city.

Do I have to talk about my sexuality or gender in therapy?

Not at all. Your identity is respected and welcomed, but sessions focus on whatever concerns you bring, whether that is work stress, relationships, anxiety, or personal growth. You set the agenda.

How do we get started with sessions?

We begin with a free 20 minute introductory call online. It is an informal way to discuss your goals, ask questions about fees or insurance, and see if my therapeutic approach suits you.
