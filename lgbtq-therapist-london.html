Finding an affirming therapist in London

Living in London offers vibrant queer spaces and diverse culture, but navigating city life can also carry unique pressures. Finding a therapist who understands minority stress, identity exploration, and queer relationship structures can take substantial time. When you are already balancing a demanding career and busy social schedule, searching for the right support across the capital can feel daunting.

Online sessions eliminate the stress of travelling across London after a long working day. Whether you live in Soho, Clapham, Hackney, Vauxhall, or outer Greater London, working online offers complete privacy, consistency, and comfort. You can settle into therapy from your own living room or private office space without adding hours of travel on the tube.

Relational, somatic and person-centred LGBTQ+ therapy

I work with adults from all backgrounds living in London, with a core specialism in LGBTQ+-affirming therapy. As a gay man, my work is rooted in lived experience as well as professional clinical training. In our sessions, you will never need to educate your therapist about queer terminology, gender identity, or non-traditional relationship models.

My therapeutic approach integrates person-centred counselling with somatic principles. Person-centred therapy provides deep empathy, warmth, and unconditional positive regard, creating a safe foundation where you can drop your guard. Combining this with somatic awareness allows us to explore how emotional stress, past shame, or societal pressure shows up physically in your body.

Questions about online LGBTQ+ therapy in London

Why choose online LGBTQ+ therapy if I live in London?

London is geographically vast, and travelling across the city for weekly appointments can add unwanted stress. Online therapy allows you to meet from the comfort and privacy of your home, saving time while ensuring you work with a specialist LGBTQ+ therapist who truly understands your background.

Will I need to explain queer terms or lifestyle to you?

No. As a gay therapist with lived experience and eight years of clinical practice, I provide a queer-informed space where you never have to explain terminology, dating culture, or identity nuances. We can focus immediately on your personal growth, relationships, and emotional wellbeing.

Can I use private health insurance for online LGBTQ+ therapy in London?

Yes, if your health insurance policy covers outpatient mental health care. I am registered with major UK health insurers including Aviva, AXA, Bupa, Healix, and Vitality. During our free 20-minute introductory call, we can discuss how to use your insurance cover for online sessions.
