Finding an affirming therapist in London

London offers vibrant queer spaces and a rich cultural scene, but navigating city life as an LGBTQ+ person can also bring distinct pressures. Finding support that feels knowledgeable about minority stress, identity exploration, or non-traditional relationships often takes significant effort. Balancing a busy professional schedule while searching for the right therapist can add unnecessary strain.

Meeting online removes the hassle of travelling across the capital after a demanding day. Wherever you are logging in from across London, online sessions provide privacy, consistency, and convenience. You can settle into your therapeutic work from a comfortable room at home or a private space without having to spend extra time navigating public transport.

Relational, somatic and person-centred LGBTQ+ therapy

I work with adults from all backgrounds living in London, with a dedicated specialism in LGBTQ+-affirming therapy. As a gay man, my practice is grounded in both professional clinical training and lived experience. You won't need to spend time explaining queer terminology, gender identity, or non-monogamous relationship structures during our sessions.

My approach combines person-centred counselling with body-aware somatic principles. Person-centred therapy provides deep empathy, warmth, and unconditional positive regard, creating a supportive environment where you can explore difficult thoughts at your own pace. Integrating somatic awareness allows us to notice how societal pressure, past shame, or relational stress may manifest physically.

Exploring identity and relationships in therapy works best when you feel genuinely seen and accepted. Having a therapist who understands the nuances of queer social dynamics, coming out, or complex family tension means we can move past surface explanations quickly, focusing on what matters most to your emotional wellbeing and growth.

Clients often tell me that half the relief of our first session was simply not having to translate their life into mainstream terms. Being understood from the first sentence changes what we can accomplish together.

Common concerns LGBTQ+ Londoners bring to therapy

LGBTQ+ Londoners reach out for support with a wide range of personal, relational, and identity-related concerns. Common themes include navigating coming out, exploring complex family dynamics or estrangement, managing workplace burnout, and addressing ongoing anxiety around dating, body image, or intimacy in a fast-paced urban environment where expectations often feel high.

Questions about online LGBTQ+ therapy in London

Why choose online LGBTQ+ therapy if I live in London?

London is geographically large, and travelling across the capital for weekly appointments can add unwanted stress. Online therapy allows you to meet from a private, comfortable space at home, saving travel time while connecting with an affirming therapist who understands queer experience.

Will I need to explain queer terms or dating culture to you?

No. As a gay therapist with lived experience and eight years of clinical practice, I offer a queer-informed space where you do not need to explain terminology, dating culture, or identity nuances. We can focus directly on your personal growth, relationships, and emotional health.

Can I use private health insurance for online LGBTQ+ therapy in London?

Yes, if your health insurance policy covers outpatient mental health care. I am registered with major UK health insurers including Aviva, AXA, Bupa, Healix, and Vitality. During our free 20 minute introductory call, we can discuss how to use your insurance cover for online sessions.
