Exploring relationship and family dynamics individually

Many people seeking support for relationship or family difficulties wonder if they need to attend therapy together with a partner or relative. I work exclusively with individuals one to one. Focusing on your individual experience can allow us to explore your feelings, boundaries, and personal history without needing to manage another person's reactions in the room.

Whether you are dealing with family estrangement, disapproval after coming out, or recurring struggles in dating and intimacy, individual sessions give you room to pause and reflect. We can look closely at old attachment patterns, emotional expectations, and communication habits, supporting you to reflect on what feels important in your connections with others.

An affirming, relational and body aware approach

As a gay therapist, I bring lived experience alongside professional training to our sessions. You will never have to explain basic queer terms, cultural context, or non-traditional relationship structures to me. That foundational understanding means we can focus directly on what matters most to you, building a warm and non-judgmental therapeutic relationship.

My practice combines person-centred counselling with somatic principles. Relationship struggles often manifest physically, such as a knot in the stomach before visiting family or tension during conflict with a partner. By noticing these bodily cues alongside your thoughts, sessions can support your system to settle while exploring how you might establish clearer emotional boundaries.

Clients often wonder how individual work can shift a family dynamic when the other person is not in the room. Usually, changing how you carry the conversation inside yourself is where the real boundary begins.

Questions about LGBTQ+ relationship and family therapy

Do you offer couples counselling or family therapy sessions?

No, I work exclusively with individuals one to one. Rather than hosting joint sessions with partners or family members, we use individual therapy to help you explore your own emotions, consider healthy boundaries, process past dynamics, and reflect on what you want from your relationships.

What kind of family issues can we talk about in individual sessions?

We can explore any relationship difficulty that affects your wellbeing. Common themes include coming out to family, managing tension or disapproval, navigating family estrangement, considering boundaries with relatives, or reflecting on old relational roles that may no longer suit you in adulthood.

How do online sessions work for relationship concerns?

Online sessions take place over a secure video link, offering a private and comfortable space to discuss sensitive personal matters. Working online means you can join from home without travel stress, fitting therapy smoothly into your weekly routine.
