A queer informed space where you never have to explain yourself

Therapy should be a place of genuine safety where you can speak openly without fear of judgment or misunderstanding. Affirming therapy goes beyond simple tolerance; it actively honours queer lives, relationships, and identities. In our sessions, your experience is recognised as valid and meaningful, creating an environment where you can explore personal concerns without needing to censor who you are or defend your way of living.

As a gay therapist, my practice is built on shared lived experience of coming out, navigating identity, managing family dynamics, and unlearning internalized shame. One of the greatest relief points for queer clients is knowing that you never have to educate your therapist about basic terminology or cultural nuances. We can jump straight into what matters most to you, saving your energy for deep, meaningful self-discovery and healing.

Navigating identity, relationships and self-esteem

Queer individuals often carry subtle emotional weight from growing up in a world that assumes heteronormativity. We frequently explore topics such as family acceptance, workplace stress, intimacy, and releasing lingering guilt. If you are seeking targeted support for personal growth, you might explore my pages on therapy for shame and self-esteem or my specific focus on online therapy for gay men.

While queer-informed work is a primary specialism of mine, I work with adults from all backgrounds and walks of life. Affirming therapy does not mean every conversation must focus on your sexuality or gender. Instead, it ensures that when identity does intersect with anxiety, work, or relationships, it is understood through an empathetic lens. We combine relational warmth with gentle body-aware somatic practices to support your overall emotional health.

Online sessions across the UK and Ireland

Whether you are seeking an LGBTQ+ therapist in London or living in a smaller community, video sessions offer complete privacy and convenience from the comfort of home.

Questions about LGBTQ+ affirming therapy

What does LGBTQ+ affirming therapy actually mean?

It means your identity and relationships are fully respected as valid and healthy. Therapy is free from judgment, subtle bias, or pathologising assumptions. We work together with an understanding of queer culture and the unique stresses that can come with living in a minority group.

Do we have to talk about my sexuality or gender in every session?

No. You choose what we discuss in every session. Whether you want to focus on career changes, anxiety, family relationships, or personal identity, your identity is simply the safe backdrop against which our work takes place.

How do I start online therapy with Peter?

Starting is simple and informal. You can book a free 20 minute introductory call to chat about your needs, ask any questions about my approach, and see how it feels to talk together before making any decisions.
