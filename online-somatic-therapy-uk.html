Understanding somatic therapy and the nervous system

Many people come to therapy having already spent years trying to think their way out of stress. While traditional talking therapies focus primarily on thoughts and narratives, somatic therapy recognises that difficult experiences leave physical footprints in the nervous system. When we experience overwhelm, our bodies store that protective energy as tension, hypervigilance, or a quiet sense of numbness. Somatic therapy works by bringing gentle attention to those physical sensations, allowing the body to complete unreleased survival responses safely.

The foundation of this approach was developed by Dr Peter Levine through Somatic Experiencing. His research demonstrated how nervous systems can become stuck in fight, flight, or freeze states long after a stressful event has ended. Rather than forcing you to relive painful memories, somatic work focuses on small, manageable sensations in the present moment. By tracking breath, posture, and subtle physical shifts, your body learns that the original threat has passed, restoring a genuine sense of internal calm and resilience.

The benefits of body-aware therapy online

A common question is whether body-aware therapy can be effective over a video connection. In practice, working online from your own home often enhances the sense of physical safety. Being in a familiar, comfortable environment helps your nervous system settle more readily than arriving at an unfamiliar clinic. Without the added stress of travelling across town, you can drop into the session feeling relaxed and transition quietly back into your day afterward.

I work with clients living throughout the United Kingdom and Ireland, providing private video sessions that bring specialist care directly to your space. Whether you are looking for somatic therapy in London or joining from a quiet rural town, the focus remains entirely on your internal experience. Through careful observation and attentive listening, I guide you in tracking physical sensations, using simple grounding exercises that you can comfortably practice in your daily life.

Questions about online somatic therapy in the UK

What happens during an online somatic therapy session?

We sit together over a secure video connection and talk through what you are experiencing. Alongside your words, we pay quiet attention to physical cues such as your breath, posture, and muscle tension. We use simple grounding techniques to help your nervous system regulate at a comfortable pace.

Is somatic therapy suitable if I have never tried therapy before?

Yes. Somatic therapy is gentle and accessible, making it an excellent choice for anyone new to therapy. There are no complicated theories to learn or emotional demands placed upon you. We start exactly where you are, keeping the work safe, transparent, and focused on your personal comfort.

How do I know if online somatic therapy is right for me?

If you feel that stress or past difficulties are showing up physically in your body, somatic work may be ideal. The best way to find out is to book a free 20 minute introductory call, where we can chat about your goals and answer any questions.
