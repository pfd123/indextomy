Finding body aware support in Belfast without the travel

Living and working in Belfast comes with its own rhythm. Whether you are navigating busy professional days, family life, or the pace of the city, carrying chronic stress can leave you feeling physically exhausted. Traditional talking therapy helps you understand your thoughts, but when anxiety stays trapped in muscle tension, restless sleep, or a racing heart, your body needs a different kind of attention.

Online sessions fit comfortably into your routine without requiring you to rush through city traffic or search for parking. You can join from a quiet room at home, wrap up in a blanket, and focus entirely on your own wellbeing. Having space right after a session to rest, rather than navigating a journey across Belfast, allows the gentle shifts in your nervous system to settle deeply.

Combining somatic experiencing with person centred therapy

My practice brings together two gentle and effective therapeutic foundations. I draw on Somatic Experiencing, a body-oriented approach created by Dr Peter Levine that helps release stored tension and regulate the nervous system. Alongside this, my work is rooted in person-centred principles of empathy, warmth, and unconditional positive regard, ensuring you are supported as a whole person rather than a collection of symptoms.

I support adults from all backgrounds, including individuals seeking dedicated online somatic therapy in the UK or targeted support for somatic therapy for anxiety. As a gay man, I also bring extensive LGBTQ+ affirming experience to my work, offering a space where identity, culture, and personal background are welcomed with complete acceptance and without any need for explanation.

Common body symptoms and nervous system patterns

Many clients living in Belfast come to therapy feeling that their minds are constantly working while their bodies remain frozen or overworked. You might notice a tight jaw, shallow breathing, digestive issues, or an overwhelming sense of fatigue that sleep does not fix. These physical signs are often how your nervous system communicates that it has been carrying too much for too long.

In our sessions, we do not force rapid changes or try to fix your experience. Instead, we pay attention to subtle physical sensations and learn to track when your body feels safe or overwhelmed. By moving at a slow and steady pace, your system learns to release old patterns of tension and regain a feeling of grounded safety in everyday life.

Questions about online somatic therapy in Belfast

How does online somatic therapy work for people in Belfast?

Online sessions take place over secure video, allowing you to join from a quiet, private space in your own home. We focus on bodily sensations, breathing, and nervous system regulation just as effectively as in person, without any travel across the city.

Do I need any previous experience with somatic therapy?

No prior experience is necessary. We start wherever you are, moving at a gentle pace that feels manageable for your body. I guide you through noticing physical sensations in a calm, supportive way.

How do I book a session from Belfast?

You can begin by arranging a free 20 minute introductory call online. We will talk through your needs, answer any questions you have about fees or insurance, and decide together if we are a good fit.
