Why somatic therapy suits fast-paced London life

Living and working in London frequently demands sustained high energy, tight schedules, and constant adaptability. Over time, this environment can keep your nervous system in a state of perpetual alertness. You might notice physical signs of stress such as tight shoulders, shallow breathing, restless sleep, or a persistent feeling of being unable to unwind, even during time off.

While traditional talking therapies can clarify your thoughts, somatic work addresses where physical stress actually sits in the body. Meeting online means you can connect from home or a quiet workspace in Soho, Clapham, Shoreditch, Vauxhall, or anywhere in Greater London. Eliminating travel time across the capital makes therapy a grounding pause rather than another rushed errand.

A person-centred, body-aware and LGBTQ+ affirming practice

My therapeutic approach combines Person-Centred counselling with Somatic Experiencing, a methodology developed by Dr Peter Levine. Person-centred therapy provides a foundation of unconditional positive regard, empathy, and authenticity, ensuring you feel truly heard and accepted. We then incorporate gentle body awareness to help your nervous system release stuck survival energy safely and gradually.

I work with clients from all walks of life, and I bring significant experience as a gay therapist. My practice is warmly LGBTQ+-affirming, offering an inclusive space where queer Londoners can explore identity, relationship patterns, and minority stress without explanation. Common concerns London clients bring to therapy

Many people living in London seek somatic therapy when talking about their worries no longer brings relief. Common issues include chronic anxiety, workplace burnout, feeling disconnected from physical sensations, or carrying unresolved relational trauma. Working somatically helps you tune into physical sensations slowly, widening your capacity to handle stress without feeling overwhelmed.

Questions about online somatic therapy in London

Do you offer in-person somatic therapy in London?

No, all sessions are conducted online. Working online allows London clients to attend therapy without travelling across the city, providing a comfortable, private environment where you can focus entirely on your nervous system and body awareness.

How does somatic therapy help with London city stress?

High-pressure city environments keep the nervous system in fight-or-flight mode. Somatic therapy tracks bodily sensations like breath and muscular tension, guiding your system out of chronic alertness so you can experience genuine rest and physical ease.

Can I use private medical insurance for somatic therapy in London?

Yes, provided your policy covers outpatient psychotherapy. I am an approved provider with Aviva, AXA, Bupa, Healix, and Vitality. We can discuss how your health insurance coverage applies during our free 20-minute introductory call.
