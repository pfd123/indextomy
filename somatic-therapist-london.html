Online Therapy for London

Online somatic therapy for clients in London.

Long commutes and packed diaries do not leave much room for a clinic across town. I work with clients across London entirely online, bringing a relational, body-aware approach to anxiety, trauma and the sense of always being switched on.

Why somatic therapy suits London life

A lot of what I hear from clients based in London is the same feeling: busy, capable, holding it together, and quietly exhausted. Talking things through helps, but it is not always enough on its own. Somatic Experiencing works with the body as much as the mind, gently noticing where tension, bracing or numbness sits, and helping the nervous system complete what it got stuck on.

Because sessions are online, there is no travel time added to an already full week. You can join from home, from work, or wherever gives you the most privacy and space to settle.

Clients across the whole of London

I work with people based right across the capital, including Soho, Clapham, Shoreditch, Vauxhall and further out into Greater London. Since everything is held online, which part of London you are in never changes how sessions work, though it is often useful context when we talk about your routine and what support might fit around it.

A few London-specific questions

Do you see clients in person in London?

No, sessions are held online, so there is no commute across the city and no clinic to travel to. Many clients prefer this, especially around a busy work day.

Is online therapy from London covered by my health insurance?

Often, yes. I am registered to work with Aviva, AXA PPP, Bupa, Healix and Vitality, and also work a couple of days each week as a Bupa therapist. Check your policy for session limits, then get in touch.

How do I know if somatic therapy is right for me?

If talking alone has not been enough, or you notice anxiety and tension showing up in your body rather than just your thoughts, somatic work is often a good fit. The free intro call is a low-pressure way to find out. See the full FAQ for more general questions.
