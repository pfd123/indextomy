Accessible online therapy across Northern Ireland

Finding the right therapist locally can sometimes feel limiting, especially if you live outside major urban centres or want a specialised approach like somatic experiencing or queer-affirming therapy. Online sessions connect us seamlessly, allowing clients to join from towns, cities or rural areas across Northern Ireland without the stress of travel or scheduling around long journeys.

Online therapy offers a quiet, personal space wherever you are in Northern Ireland. Whether you are managing a demanding career, family responsibilities, or rural routines, having sessions online allows you to integrate therapeutic care into your weekly life easily. Afterwards, you can remain in your comfortable surroundings to process and rest.

By removing the need to travel, online sessions make it easier to maintain regular appointments alongside work and family life. You can join from a private space at home, creating a consistent environment where you can step back from daily demands and focus on your emotional wellbeing at a steady, manageable pace.

A holistic approach: somatic, person centred and LGBTQ+ affirming

My therapeutic practice integrates person-centred principles with somatic body awareness. Person-centred therapy, founded on empathy and non-judgmental acceptance, ensures that your experience is heard and respected. Somatic Experiencing, developed by Dr Peter Levine, offers a way to explore anxiety and nervous system tension by paying gentle attention to physical sensations and held stress.

I support adults facing a wide variety of personal challenges. As a gay man, I also provide an experienced, welcoming space for LGBTQ+ individuals across the region. If you are looking for location specific details, you can explore my pages for online somatic therapy in Belfast or my work as an online LGBTQ+ therapist in Belfast.

Clients outside major cities often tell me how exhausting it was trying to find specialist therapy nearby. Working online allows us to focus entirely on body awareness and emotional grounding without worrying about long drives or local privacy.

Common reasons clients seek therapy

People across Northern Ireland reach out for therapy for many reasons. You might be experiencing persistent anxiety, physical symptoms of chronic stress, burnout from work, or challenges in relationships. Others come to work through feelings of shame, low self-esteem, life transitions, or to explore self-acceptance in a safe, non-judgmental environment.

Therapy is a collaborative process where we pay attention to both mind and body. Rather than offering quick fixes or rigid diagnoses, we work at a steady, manageable pace that respects your unique history. Together, we cultivate body awareness and explore held tension at a manageable pace, supporting a gradual move towards greater ease and personal resilience.
