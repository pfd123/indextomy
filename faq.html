Common Questions

Things people ask before we begin.

A few honest answers about how therapy with me works. If your question isn't here, the free intro call is the easiest way to ask it.

Do you accept health insurance?

Yes. I'm registered to work with Aviva, AXA PPP, Bupa, Healix and Vitality. Check your policy for session limits, then get in touch and I can provide anything your insurer needs.

Are sessions online or in person?

Sessions are held online across the UK, so location is never a barrier. Ecotherapy sessions are held outdoors and arranged directly with me.

Do I need a referral to start?

No referral is needed. You can self-refer directly, starting with a free 20-minute introductory call.

Is what I share confidential?

Yes. Everything you share stays confidential, in line with BACP ethical guidelines and GDPR, other than rare safeguarding exceptions I'd always discuss with you first.

How much does a session cost?

Sessions are 50 minutes at £90. The first introductory call, around 20 minutes, is free.

Where are most of your clients based?

Clients join from across the UK and Ireland, including Belfast, Dublin, London, Manchester and Edinburgh. Sessions are held online, so location is never a barrier.

Do you only work with LGBTQ+ clients?

No. I work with adults from all backgrounds. Queer-informed practice is one of my specialisms, not a limit on who I work with.

How long have you been a therapist?

I have eight years of experience working as a therapist, alongside ongoing training in somatic and ecotherapy approaches.

Not sure which approach is right for you? That's exactly what the intro call is for. We talk it through together, no pressure, no obligation.
